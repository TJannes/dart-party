# Animation ideas

What is agreed about animations is in `PLAN.md` under "Animations", with the
build steps 6, 8 and 9. This file only keeps ideas that are not yet decided.

The themed ideas below are a starting point. Each game's ideas are put to
the owner when that game is about to be done (step 8).

## Themed moments per game

**Race** (race track)
- Forward: dust cloud and speed lines; triple = nitro flame.
- Pushed back: a banana peel or oil slick thrown by the attacker; the victim
  spins backwards.
- Finish: chequered flag, finish tape snaps, camera flash.

**Robin Hood** (archery in the forest)
- Darts land as arrows that quiver in the target.
- Bullseye: an arrow splits the previous arrow, feathers fly.
- Low zone: the arrow thuds into a tree, a bird flies up.

**Spotlight** (stage)
- New round: the spotlight sweeps over the board with a drum roll and stops.
- Hit the target: the avatar stands in the beam and bows, roses are thrown.
- Miss it: the beam swings away, a single cough from the audience.

**Piggy Bank**
- Coins fall into the pig, which grows fatter with the total and wiggles.
- Off the board: a hammer comes down, the pig cracks, half the coins roll
  away. The pig gets a plaster and looks grumpy.
- Big dart: a golden coin, the pig grins.

**Land Grab** (castles)
- Claim: a flag in the player's colour is planted, a castle rises.
- Fortify: a second wall is built stone by stone.
- Attack: a catapult stone flies from the attacker; the wall shows a crack.
- Capture: the wall crumbles, the old flag drops, the new one goes up.

**Sniper**
- New round: the cross drops in like a scope locking on.
- Dart: a dotted line measures the distance to the cross, millimetres count
  up.
- Round result: the three closest darts get gold, silver and bronze rings.

**Minefield** (treasure island)
- Reveal: a shovel digs the segment open, a pause for suspense.
- Chest: the lid opens, a glow and coins. Coin: a single flip.
- Bomb: explosion, the avatar blackened with messy hair, half the points
  blown away.
- Empty: a tumbleweed or a small crab walks out.

**Last One Standing** (arena)
- Hit someone: a boxing glove on a spring punches the victim's avatar.
- Own segment: the glove turns round and hits the thrower.
- Out: the avatar is counted out with stars around its head.
- Two left: "Final duel" banner, the lights dim.

**Higher or Lower** (card table)
- The call flips in as a big arrow, up or down, like a card being turned.
- Right: the arrow turns green and shoots onward.
- Wrong: the arrow breaks, a buzzer, a heart cracks.

**Time Bomb**
- The bomb sits with the player at the board; its fuse sparks.
- Every burning dart: the fuse shortens visibly and the ticking speeds up,
  the avatar sweats more and more.
- Target hit: the avatar tosses the bomb to the next player with relief.
- Boom: full-screen flash, the avatar blackened, a life falls off.

**Raise the Bar** (high jump)
- The bar is a real high-jump bar at the height of the total.
- Over it: the avatar jumps over, the bar moves up, the pot gets a coin.
- Not over it: the avatar knocks the bar off; the holder collects the pot.

**Draw, standings, deciding round**
- Draw: the chosen game bursts out of the reel with its own icon.
- Deciding round: "Sudden death" curtain, the tied avatars face each other.

## General moments, as a starting point for step 6

| Moment | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| Dart lands | Impact ring and a little shake on the board at the exact spot; the value flies from the dart to the player's score. |
| Good dart | Avatar hops, sparkles in the player's colour. |
| Great dart | Scene flashes, avatar cheers, confetti burst from the avatar. |
| Bad dart / miss | Avatar sulks, a small grey cloud above it, the dart "boings" off the edge. |
| Attack | Something flies from the attacker's avatar to the victim's avatar; the victim flinches. |
| Life lost | A heart cracks and falls off the player's card. Last life: the heart pulses beforehand. |
| Knocked out | Avatar turns grey, spins and drops off the screen, or gets a ghost that floats up. |
| Turn change | The next avatar slides to the oche under a spotlight; name banner sweeps in. |
| Game won | Winner's avatar grows to the centre, confetti cannon, the others clap or sulk. |
| Points handed out | Points fly as coins into the scoreboard rows; rows swap places with a slide. |
| Podium | Avatars climb their step one by one, third first; fireworks for the winner. |
| Three in one segment | "Hat-trick!" banner. |
| Bullseye | The board pulses outward like a gong. |
| Triple 20 / 180 | Fireworks, the lights of the game-show frame chase. |
| Three misses in one turn | Tumbleweed rolls past, a cricket chirps. |
| Decided on the last dart | Slow-motion zoom on that dart. |

## Later

- Streaks: a flame on the avatar after three good turns in a row; it fizzles
  out with a puff on a bad one.
- Comeback, from last to first in one game: a rocket under the avatar.
- A polished version of one game, to compare with the simple one.
